Murtaza SEO Tools

A collection of browser-based tools for cleaning rich text into editable HTML, resizing and converting images, and counting words or characters. Your files stay on your device.

Rich Text → Clean HTML

Paste formatted text or upload images. The clean HTML preview updates automatically as you edit.

Keeps headings, bold and italic text, links, images, lists, tables, and useful formatting. Removes unnecessary paragraph, div, and span wrappers.

HTML updates automatically as you type or paste.

Batch Image Format Converter

Convert multiple images to PNG, JPEG, JPG, or WebP and download each file individually or together as a ZIP.

Files are processed locally in your browser. Animated GIFs convert as a still image.

Choose images to begin.

Image Resizer

Resize an image to exact pixel dimensions, then preview and download the result.

The original aspect ratio can be locked. The output format is preserved for JPEG, PNG, and WebP files.

Choose an image to begin.

Count words, characters, characters without spaces, sentences, and paragraphs as you type or paste text.
