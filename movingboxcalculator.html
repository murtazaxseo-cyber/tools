    Step 03 — Auditable math

    Open the formula

    No black boxes — only the cardboard kind. Every figure your manifest produces can be traced to one of five documented stages.

    1. 1

      Footage baseline

      Shared space — living rooms, hall closets, decor — scales with square footage: per 100 sq ft we allocate 0.35 book boxes, 0.70 medium, 0.32 large and 0.18 XL. A living room is mostly volume, not count, so larger homes add bulk (L/XL) faster than count (S).

    2. 2

      Room crate loads

      Kitchens, bedrooms, bathrooms and each additional occupant contribute fixed allocations drawn from mover benchmarks: a bedroom is 2.5S/4M/1.5L/1XL, a bathroom 1.5S/1M, the kitchen 5S/6M/2L. Optional spaces — office, garage, shed — append their own loads.

    3. 3

      Specialty passthrough

      Wardrobe boxes derive from linear closet feet (1 box = 2 ft), dish packs from place settings (1 each), book boxes from shelves (¾ each), picture sleeves and TV kits from explicit counts. These bypass every density multiplier: a full closet is a full closet.

    4. 4

      Behavioral modulation

      Two factors tune the general-purpose base: lifestyle density (minimalist ×0.82, standard ×1.00, maximalist ×1.22) and accumulation over time (+3% per year lived, capped at 15 years). Specialty rows stay untouched.

    5. 5

      Buffer & rounding

      Everything is summed per box class, scaled by your overpack buffer (0–20%) and rounded up. The buffer is deliberate: real moves always surface one more drawer. Result: eight honest integer counts you can actually buy.

    Why wardrobes ignore density +

    Closet volume is measured directly in linear feet, so lifestyle is already priced in: a maximalist simply declares more closet feet. Multiplying again would double-count the signal.

    Where the cost range comes from +

    Each class carries a current U.S. retail price. The low end (−15%) models dollar-store or second-hand cartons; the high end (+25%) models premium moving-supply kits with double-wall construction.

    Why furniture is excluded +

    Volume and vehicle recommendations cover boxes only — the part you can count and buy. Sofas, mattresses and appliances vary too wildly to estimate from room counts; your carrier will survey them.

    Step 04 — Sequence the chaos

    The 4-week packing countdown

    Boxes are useless in the wrong order. Run your manifest four weeks out, then pack from coldest storage to warmest — the kettle boxes come last for a reason.

    1. T−4

      weeks to haul-day

      Audit & acquire

      Run the tally, order the full manifest, and start purging. Every box you own should already have a destination room.

      • Order boxes, tape, paper in one delivery
      • Donate or sell the lightest 20%
      • Photograph fragile and high-value items

    2. T−3

      weeks to haul-day

      Cold storage first

      Pack what you won't touch for a month: seasonal gear, books, decor, guest rooms, the garage. Label two sides, always.

      • Books into book boxes only — 65 lb cap
      • Garage tools drained and bundled
      • Seasonal linens in XL cartons

    3. T−2

      weeks to haul-day

      Kitchen flanks

      Build dish packs bottom-up with 4 inches of cushioning. Keep one 'open first' kit per person and a hardware bag per furniture piece.

      • One dish pack per place setting
      • Tape X across mirror and glass faces
      • Cords photographed, then bagged and boxed

    4. T−1

      weeks to haul-day

      Essential lanes

      Everything left should fit in a suitcase plus six boxes. Wardrobe boxes go up last and come down first — they double as moving closets.

      • Wardrobe: 2 linear ft per box, shoes below
      • Bedding in XL, marked 'make beds first'
      • Essentials: chargers, meds, kettle, tape, cutter

    Haul-day rule — load order: XL and wardrobe first, book boxes last, fragile verticals strapped.

    Step 05 — Clear customs

    Questions, stamped & answered

    The doubts every mover has the night before the tape runs out.

    Still unsure?

    The fastest way to validate the estimate is to pack one room and compare counts — the model predicts roughly 2 book boxes, 4 medium, 1 large and 1 XL per bedroom before density factors.

    Re-run the tally ↑
    01How accurate is the moving box estimate?+
    The model anchors to industry packing benchmarks for square footage and room type, then applies your lifestyle density, years lived and specialty items. Most households land within ±10% of the estimate — the overpack buffer absorbs the rest. If you drift toward maximalist on the lifestyle dial, budget up.
    02Why does wardrobe count ignore my lifestyle setting?+
    Hanging clothes are measured in linear feet, so the wardrobe count comes straight from your closet footage: one wardrobe box carries exactly 2 linear feet of garments. Density and years-lived multipliers only affect general-purpose boxes, because closet volume barely changes once a wardrobe is full.
    03Does the total volume include my furniture?+
    No. The cubic-footage figure is boxes only — the part you can actually count. Furniture, mattresses and appliances add their own volume, which is why the truck recommendation assumes the boxes fill roughly 60–70% of the suggested vehicle when you load sensibly.
    04What is a dish pack and why is it separate?+
    A dish pack (or china barrel) is a double-walled 5.2 cu ft box rated for 75 lbs. Each one holds roughly one 8-piece place setting with glassware, so we generate one per place setting you declare — fragile kitchenware should never ride in a general-purpose carton.
    05How is the cost range calculated?+
    We price every box class at current U.S. big-box retail, add the full supplies manifest (tape, paper, bubble wrap, pads, labels), then frame it with a ±15–25% band. Buying second-hand cartons or renting crates lands you near the low end; premium moving-store kits near the high end.
    06How do I share my estimate?+
    Tap Share link in the manifest panel. Your exact configuration is encoded into the page URL (no account, no server), so anyone who opens the link sees the calculator pre-loaded with your numbers. The page also remembers your draft between visits on the same device.
    07What if I have already packed some rooms?+
    Drop the overpack buffer to 0% and dial the room counts down with the steppers — bedrooms, shelves and closets all accept zero. The room-by-room breakdown shows exactly where every box is expected to come from, so you can compare against what is already taped.
